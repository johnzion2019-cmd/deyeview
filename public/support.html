Store review and Demo Mode

DeyeView opens with two choices. Try Live Demo runs a full plant on the device: a ticking energy flow, battery runtime, day and month savings, 30-day history, records, payback, a solar forecast, CSV export, and Settings. No inverter and no Deye account are required. A “Demo Mode · Connect Inverter” pill stays visible until a real inverter is connected.

Connect a Deye inverter

  1. Create or open the Deye Cloud account you already use in the Deye app, and note whether it lives in the EU or US datacenter.
  2. At developer.deyecloud.com, create an application and copy the App ID and App Secret.
  3. In DeyeView, choose Connect My Deye Inverter. Pick EMEA / APAC (eu1-developer.deyecloud.com) or Americas (us1-developer.deyecloud.com).
  4. Enter the App ID, App Secret, Deye email, password, and inverter serial number, then the tariff, battery, and location. Test & connect signs in from the phone.

The password is SHA-256 hashed on the device before it is sent. A 64-character hex digest pasted from another tool is sent unchanged. Credentials are not stored on a DeyeView server.

Delete data from this phone

Open Settings and tap Disconnect & Clear Local Data. That is the deletion control for Apple and Google data-deletion requirements. DeyeView does not keep an account that support staff can erase remotely. To close the Deye Cloud account itself, use Deye’s own process.

Forecast

Today and tomorrow’s estimated kWh come from Open-Meteo shortwave radiation for the coordinates you enter, scaled to the inverter’s rated power. If the weather service cannot be reached, DeyeView shows a clearly labeled on-device estimate.
