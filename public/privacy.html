DeyeView

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · DeyeView 1.0.0

DeyeView is an independent companion for people who own a Deye hybrid inverter. It is not operated by Ningbo Deye Inverter Technology Co., Ltd. The app is free at launch, shows no ads, and does not create a DeyeView account.

What stays on your device

Your App ID, App Secret, Deye email, password, inverter serial number, access token, plant name, currency, electricity rate, billing cycle, battery size, install cost, city, coordinates, time zone, and optional house photo are stored in on-device app preferences. DeyeView does not upload that information to a server we operate, because we do not operate one.

What leaves your device

  • Deye Cloud. When you connect an inverter, the app talks directly from your phone to the region you choose: eu1-developer.deyecloud.com (EMEA / APAC) or us1-developer.deyecloud.com (Americas). It sends the App ID, App Secret, email, and a SHA-256 hash of your password to /v1.0/account/token, then uses the returned token to read /v1.0/device/latest and /v1.0/station/history for your serial number. Deye’s own privacy policy applies to that account.
  • Open-Meteo. The forecast requests weather for the latitude, longitude, and time zone you enter from api.open-meteo.com. Open-Meteo does not require an account. No inverter credentials are included.

Demo Mode does not contact Deye Cloud. It may still request the Open-Meteo forecast for the demo city. If that request fails, the app shows an on-device estimate.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, run advertising, use tracking SDKs, or collect analytics. The house photo is not transmitted. Tariff and install-cost figures are not transmitted. There is no DeyeView login we can reset, because the only login is the one you hold with Deye.

Delete your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Disconnect & Clear Local Data. That removes the saved login, token, tariff, house photo, and demo choice from the device. We have no server copy to erase. If your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) includes app data, clear that backup with the platform’s tools as well.

Children

DeyeView is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
