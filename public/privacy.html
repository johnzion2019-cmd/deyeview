DeyeView

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · DeyeView 1.0.0

DeyeView is an independent companion for people who own a Deye hybrid inverter. It is not operated by Ningbo Deye Inverter Technology Co., Ltd. The app is free, does not create a DeyeView account, and may show one Google AdMob banner on the Android and iOS dashboard.

What stays on your device

Your App ID, App Secret, Deye email, password, inverter serial number, access token, plant name, currency, electricity rate, billing cycle, battery size, install cost, city, coordinates, time zone, and optional house photo are stored in on-device app preferences. DeyeView does not upload that information to a server we operate, because we do not operate one.

What leaves your device

  • Deye Cloud. When you connect an inverter, the app talks directly from your phone to the region you choose: eu1-developer.deyecloud.com (EMEA / APAC) or us1-developer.deyecloud.com (Americas). It sends the App ID, App Secret, email, and a SHA-256 hash of your password to /v1.0/account/token, then uses the returned token to read /v1.0/device/latest and /v1.0/station/history for your serial number. Deye’s own privacy policy applies to that account.
  • Open-Meteo. The forecast requests weather for the latitude, longitude, and time zone you enter from api.open-meteo.com. Open-Meteo does not require an account. No inverter credentials are included.

Demo Mode does not contact Deye Cloud. It may still request the Open-Meteo forecast for the demo city. If that request fails, the app shows an on-device estimate.

Advertising

On the Android and iOS apps, the main dashboard can show one banner at the bottom of the screen through Google AdMob. The browser preview, the welcome screen, and Settings do not show ads. Before the first ad request, the app asks Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent and, on iPhone and iPad, shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline tracking or personalized ads, DeyeView requests non-personalized ads. If you are offline or Google has no ad to show, the banner area stays closed.

AdMob may receive device and advertising identifiers, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information. That data is used to show and measure ads and, if you allow tracking, to make them more relevant. Google’s policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy. Reopen the choice any time from Settings → Privacy & ad choices. A future optional Pro purchase is planned to remove ads. It is not for sale in this version, and a banner is not requested when ads are turned off on the device.

The app-ads.txt file for the public site will live at https://deyeview.onrender.com/app-ads.txt once an AdMob publisher id exists. Until then that file is a placeholder.

What we do not do

We do not sell your inverter login, tariff, or house photo. The house photo is not transmitted. Tariff and install-cost figures are not transmitted. There is no DeyeView login we can reset, because the only login is the one you hold with Deye. Phone advertising is limited to the Google AdMob banner described above.

Delete your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Disconnect & Clear Local Data. That removes the saved login, token, tariff, house photo, and demo choice from the device. We have no server copy to erase. If your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) includes app data, clear that backup with the platform’s tools as well.

Children

DeyeView is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
